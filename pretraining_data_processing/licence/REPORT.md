# Licence filter for a commercial-use Common Pile v0.1 subset

Problem: a record's metadata licence (CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC0, PD, GFDL) can be wrong for the record's own text. This is licence laundering (Common Pile paper, arXiv 2506.05209, Sec. 2.1 and App. C.1). The filter reads each document's own text and finds statements that restrict the work. An inline statement overrides the metadata licence.

Code: `licence_filter.py` (`classify(doc) -> (action, reasons, spans)`; `apply_action(doc, row)` rewrites one record from one actions row). Check: `test_licence_filter.py` runs `classify` on 136 full real records (fixtures in `test_docs.jsonl.gz`, addressed by shard, record ordinal and sha1) and checks the actions-row format on two real records that share one id; exit code 1 on failure. Per-source outputs: `<source>_actions.jsonl` (one row per non-keep record: shard, ordinal = 0-based record index in the shard, sha1 of the text, id, action, reasons, spans) and `<source>_summary.json` (counts, examples, regexes). Ids repeat inside shards with different texts, so a rewrite must address records by (shard, ordinal) and check the sha1. Spans are [start, end) offsets in Python `str` code points; `apply_strip` removes them.

## Decision rule

A line is examined when it contains an NC/ND token, "all rights reserved", a permission phrase, "no part of this ... may be reproduced", or a restricted-use phrase ("no permission is granted for commercial", "personal use only", "educational purposes and personal use", "not distributed or used commercially", "not for commercial use"). NC/ND tokens: NonCommercial, NoDerivatives, BY-NC / BY-ND / BY-SA-ND, "CC NC" / "CC NC SA", creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc|by-nd. A token counts only with a Creative Commons context (CC, CCBY, Creative Commons, creativecommons, Attribution) within 150 chars, or in the BY-NC / BY-ND form. "commercial and non-commercial" is removed first. Permission phrases: "used/reused/reprinted/reproduced/included/printed/published/adapted" followed within 5 words by "with/by (the) (kind/express/written) (copyright) permission"; "with (the) permission of" when the line also has ©, "material from" or "excerpt".

Each examined line goes through these tests in order; the first test that matches decides.

Document-level rules (category a or 3, drop the document):

- metadata licence missing, or NC/ND/ARR (`metadata_licence_not_commercial`).
- Project Gutenberg: "COPYRIGHTED Project Gutenberg" header; a restricted-use phrase with a rights word within 150 chars anywhere in the eBook (World Library Shakespeare etexts: "personal use only", "not distributed or used commercially"); a copyright year after 1930 in the first 15,000 chars on a line without "Gutenberg", without a discussion word and without "not copyright", unless the year follows "renew" and the line or the 4 non-blank lines above carry a copyright year of 1930 or earlier ("Copyright, 1904 ... Copyright renewed, 1932"), and unless the book itself (outside the PG licence boilerplate) says "not renewed", "no evidence ... renewal", "evidence that ... renewed" (a "U.S." inside the sentence is allowed), "did not uncover any evidence", "renewal has not been found", "rule 6", "now in the public domain", "will be in the public domain" or "public domain in the USA/U.S./United States". Printed-source ARR and permission lines are not used for PG.
- Wikiquote main namespace: title disambiguator (film), (YYYY film), (TV series), (... TV series), (season N), (miniseries), (video game), (musical), (band), (album), (song), or a `/Season N` subpage (`wikiquote_film_tv_music_title`); a heading line Taglines / Cast / Voice cast / Seasons / Season N or one..ten / Series N / Quotes by season / Episodes / Main characters, or `<hr width="50%">` dialogue separators, or a "Directed/Written/Produced/Created by" line (bullets allowed) in the first 3,000 chars plus 5 or more `* Name: line` dialogue lines (`wikiquote_film_tv_quote_collection`); a heading line "Song lyrics" or "Lyrics" (`wikiquote_song_lyrics_section`); the lead (first 800 chars after the title line) says "X is/was a/an/the [up to 8 words without known/who/by/for/of/in/actor/director/writer/character/... ] KIND" (`wikiquote_modern_work_lead`): KIND film, movie, sitcom, miniseries, web series, anime, soap opera, video game, cartoon/animated series, or television/TV (up to 2 words) series/programme/program/show/special/film/serial/sitcom/drama, when the lead sentence has no year or its first year is after 1930; KIND "game" with the same year test and 3 or more dialogue lines (`* Name: line`, `* [action] Name: line`); KIND novel, novella, graphic novel or manga (or a "(novel)" / "(... novel)" title disambiguator) when the first year in the lead sentence is after 1930.
- Song lyrics outside PG, PDR and Wikisource (`predominantly_song_lyrics`): 3 or more verse/chorus/bridge marker lines, at least one "Verse" marker, more than 50% of lines under 60 chars; or more than 60% of non-blank lines under 60 chars and 8 or more short lines that follow cue lines "lyrics of the song", "song lyrics:", "Lyrics:", "transcription of the song".

A document with any category a or 3 reason is dropped. Otherwise a document with category b lines is stripped.

Strip span (category b): the credit clause, not the whole line. The line is split into sentences (abbreviations such as Fig., et al., Inc. and initials do not end a sentence; "2.0As" is a boundary). The span starts at the credit anchor inside the sentence of the first restriction token (Image by / Photo credit / Figure N / "Title" by / Credit: / Source: / Courtesy of / ©), else at that sentence's start, and extends back over preceding short credit sentences (max 150 chars, with a media or credit word, or "Title by Name"). It ends at the end of the last token's sentence, extended over following short credit sentences; when more than 120 chars of glued prose follow the licence token in that sentence, it ends after the licence token. When the rest of the line is at most 150 chars or fewer than 6 words (for example the title of a Lumen media item), the whole line and its newline are removed. A span longer than 500 chars (`MAX_STRIP_SPAN`) is not stripped and the line is kept (`media_credit_too_long_to_strip`). When a stripped credit has a table label (Table N), the `|` table rows directly above and directly below the credit line are removed too (`restricted_table_rows`): up to 2 non-row lines may sit between rows (spilled cells), at most 20,000 chars on each side.

Decided by the user (2026-10-10): option (a), any third-party permission text drops the whole document. Background: partial third-party content. Step 16 drops the whole document for any permission line that covers text, also when it covers only one unit (an exam question "used with the permission of the Educational Testing Service", one DOAB chapter "Reprinted with permission of Rabobank" / "adapted with permission from ... IISD"). This conflicts with the stated rule "drop only when the doc's own text restricts the whole work or the doc is predominantly third-party". The two options are: (a) state "any third-party permission text drops the document" as the rule, or (b) add span removal for self-contained reprinted units. Measured: libretexts #58137, #17414, pressbooks #44907; about 13 of the 35 drops in doab 00005 (#3399, #4857, #13809, #15514, #17009, #20118, #22023, #23856, #24743, #29065, #34156, ...).

## Known limits

- Regex heuristics, not a legal review. Precision was checked by reading examples, not measured on a labelled sample.
- Filter versions: the project_gutenberg and wikimedia rows below come from the round-2 filter (licence_filter.py md5 bc08301a0f33b911db0350f534835f8d, all shards). The other rows come from the filter before round 2. Round-2 effect measured on fresh Hugging Face copies of shards of the rewritten sources (old action -> new action, changed docs only): oercommons 00000: strip->drop 10, keep->drop 6, drop->keep 6, strip->keep 8, keep->strip 2, drop->strip 1; libretexts 00000: strip->drop 30, keep->drop 27, drop->strip 14, keep->strip 13, drop->keep 6, strip->keep 6; pressbooks 00000: drop->strip 119, drop->keep 80, strip->keep 25, strip->drop 16, keep->strip 6, keep->drop 4; pressbooks 00001: drop->strip 6, strip->drop 5, keep->strip 4, keep->drop 3, strip->keep 8, drop->keep 1; doab 00005: drop->strip 13, drop->keep 4, keep->drop 1, strip->keep 1.
- In-place state: oercommons, libretexts, pressbooks and doab were rewritten in place with the filter before the round-2 fixes (`licence_applied.json`, filter_sha256 18488a6c...). Their summaries and their shards in raw/common_pile do not include the round-2 fixes. The effect of the fixes on these sources was measured only on fresh Hugging Face copies of oercommons 00000, libretexts 00000, pressbooks 00000-00001 and doab 00005. Docs that the old filter dropped are no longer in the rewritten shards, and docs that it stripped have lost those spans; a correct result needs the original shards again.
- DOAB: a permission line that is not marked as a caption, is longer than 150 chars, does not start with the permission phrase and does not cite a journal still drops the book ("Reprinted from Toxicologic Pathology, ... Reprinted by Permission of SAGE Publications."). Peter Lang front matter "(c) Peter Lang ... All rights reserved" drops the book. A caption longer than 600 chars with a permission credit is kept unstripped (`long_line_without_strong_declaration`).
- Restricted table credits (all sources): the `|` rows directly above or below the credit are removed. Table rows that are not `|` rows (PDF text in DOAB), or that are separated from the credit by more than 2 other lines, stay in the text.
- A permission credit in textbook sources (oercommons, libretexts, pressbooks) without media evidence drops the document, because such credits often cover reproduced text (poems, readings). Partial third-party content drops the whole document (open decision above).
- A standalone `"Title" by Name is licensed under CC BY-NC` line in the middle of a body, without a media word, drops the document (pressbooks #11284 credits a diagram this way). In pressbooks 00000, 35 drops have only such lines; they credit images, videos, H5P quizzes and texts, so no rule separates them.
- Singular "map", "plate" and "table" are media words only with a number. A worksheet credit such as "Persuasion Map. Copyright 2013 IRA/NCTE. All rights reserved." now drops the page.
- Licence-teaching pages: a bare licence line, list item or table row on such a page is kept as discussion, also when it is the page's own licence.
- Resource-list detection uses the page title and URL only. A resource list with another title drops on an NC catalogue line.
- Common Core / NGSS standards notices are kept; quoted standard codes stay in the text.
- Wikiquote: pages about people that quote modern in-copyright works are kept, except musician and band pages with a lyrics heading. Pages with a "Lyrics" heading about themes (weekday pages) or public-domain songs are dropped. The lead rule uses the first year in the first 2 lead sentences; a modern film or novel whose lead names only an older year (a remake "of the 1925 novel") is kept.
- Wikimedia non-content pages (talk, user, project, help, template, file) are not dropped for ARR or permission lines and are not stripped; NC/ND declaration lines on them still drop the page. A namespace policy for these pages is a separate step.
- Project Gutenberg: books with a post-1930 copyright notice and no clearance statement in the text are dropped; PG cleared many of them as not renewed.
- Public-domain status uses the US rule (published in or before 1930). Other countries can differ.
